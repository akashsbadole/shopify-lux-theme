# Luxe Theme - User Documentation & Customization Guide

Welcome to the Luxe Theme! This guide will help you customize your store to match your brand and take full advantage of our conversion-optimized features.

## Getting Started

Luxe is built for **Shopify Online Store 2.0**, meaning every page is section-based. You can add, remove, and reorder sections on any page using the Shopify Theme Editor.

### Accessing the Theme Editor
1. Log in to your Shopify Admin.
2. Go to **Online Store > Themes**.
3. Click the **Customize** button next to your Luxe theme.

---

## Typography & Colors

### Theme Settings > Colors
Set your brand's primary and secondary colors here.
- **Brand Blue**: Used for key accents.
- **Accent Purple**: Used for primary Call-to-Action (CTA) buttons to ensure high visibility.
- **Page Background**: Control the overall mood of your store.

### Theme Settings > Typography
Luxe utilizes the **Shopify Font Picker**, giving you access to hundreds of premium and system fonts while maintaining fast load times.
- **Heading Font**: Set the font for all your H1-H6 titles.
- **Body Font**: Set the font for paragraphs, navigation, and small text.
- **Base Font Size**: Adjust the global scale of your text.

---

## Key Homepage Sections

### Hero Slider
The Hero Slider is your first impression.
- **Blocks**: Add "Slide" blocks to create a rotating gallery.
- **Optimization**: The first slide is automatically optimized for speed with `fetchpriority="high"`.
- **Settings**: Control autoplay speed, navigation dots, and arrow visibility.

### Featured Collection
Display your best products in a grid.
- **Layout**: Choose how many products to show per row and how many rows.
- **Badges**: Toggle "New" and "Sale" badges.
- **Swatches**: Enable color swatches to let users see options directly from the grid.

### Image with Text
A versatile section for storytelling.
- **Architecture**: This section uses **Blocks**. You can add multiple headings, text areas, or buttons in any order.
- **Positioning**: Swap the image and text alignment (left/right).

---

## Product Page Optimization

### Main Product Section
The heart of your store.
- **App Blocks**: This section supports **App Blocks**. You can install reviews, loyalty programs, or sizing apps and place them exactly where you want within the section.
- **Gallery**: Supports high-quality images and video.
- **Sticky Add to Cart**: Keeps the purchase button visible as the user scrolls.

### Marketing Features
Enable these in Theme Settings to boost conversion:
- **Recently Viewed**: Automatically tracks and displays products the customer has looked at.
- **Wishlist**: Allow users to save favorites (synced via local storage).
- **Quick View**: A modal that lets users view product details without leaving the collection page.

---

## Collection & Search

### Filtering & Sorting
- **OS 2.0 Filtering**: Configure your filters in **Online Store > Navigation** in your Shopify Admin. Luxe will automatically display them in a slide-out panel.
- **Layout Toggle**: Users can switch between 3-column, 4-column, and list views.

---

## Specialized Features (India & Global)

- **Pincode Checker**: (Optional) Allow users to check delivery availability by entering their PIN/ZIP code.
- **Cash on Delivery (COD) Badge**: Display trust indicators for COD availability.
- **EMI Calculator**: Show estimated monthly installments on product pages.
- **WhatsApp Chat**: A floating button to connect directly with your customers.

---

## Performance Tips

1. **Use WebP Images**: Shopify automatically converts images, but uploading high-quality assets ensures the best results.
2. **Limit Fonts**: Stick to 1-2 font families via the Font Picker for optimal speed.
3. **App Management**: Since Luxe supports App Blocks, avoid adding custom scripts to `theme.liquid`. Use App Blocks whenever possible for cleaner code and faster performance.
