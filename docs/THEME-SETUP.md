# Luxe Theme Setup Guide

Welcome to the Luxe Shopify Theme! This guide will help you set up your store for success using our premium features.

## 1. General Setup
- **Favicon**: Upload your brand's favicon in **Theme Settings > Favicon**.
- **Colors**: Customize your brand colors in **Theme Settings > Colors**. We recommend using high-contrast colors for accessibility.
- **Typography**: Adjust font sizes in **Theme Settings > Typography**.

## 2. Header & Navigation
- **Logo**: Upload your logo in the **Header section**. You can adjust its width for desktop and mobile.
- **Sticky Header**: Enable or disable the sticky header in the Header section settings.
- **Announcement Bar**: Set up your promotional messages in **Theme Settings > Header**.

## 3. Homepage Sections
- **Hero Slider**: Add high-quality images and compelling CTAs. You can enable auto-play and adjust transition speeds.
- **Featured Collection**: Show off your best products. Enable "Show view all" to encourage browsing.
- **Recently Viewed**: Add the "Recently Viewed" section to your homepage to help customers return to products they've seen.

## 4. Product Page Features
- **Sticky Add to Cart**: This bar appears as customers scroll down, keeping the purchase option always visible.
- **Urgency Indicators**: Enable stock counters and "viewing now" counts to create a sense of urgency.
- **Frequently Bought Together**: Set up bundles via product metafields (`luxe.bundle_products`) or select a fallback collection.
- **Size Guide**: Upload your size charts and link them to the size guide modal.

## 5. Performance Tips
- **Image Optimization**: Always upload images in the recommended sizes. The theme handles responsive delivery.
- **App Management**: Keep your app count low for the best performance. The Luxe theme includes many features built-in (like Wishlist and Quick View) to reduce your reliance on third-party apps.
