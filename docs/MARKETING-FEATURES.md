# Marketing & Conversion Features

Luxe Theme is designed to maximize your store's conversion rate. Here is how to use our built-in marketing tools.

## 🚀 Conversion Boosters

### 1. Sticky Add to Cart
**What it is**: A floating bar that appears when the main "Add to Cart" button scrolls out of view.
**How to use**: Enabled by default on all product pages. Ensure your product titles are concise for the best look on mobile.

### 2. AJAX Cart Upsell
**What it is**: Recommended products shown directly within the cart drawer.
**How to use**: The theme uses Shopify's Recommendation API to show relevant products based on what's currently in the cart. Ensure you have related products set up in your Shopify admin.

### 3. Frequently Bought Together (FBT)
**What it is**: A bundle section on the product page that allows customers to add multiple items to their cart at once.
**How to set up**:
- **Metafields**: Add comma-separated product handles to the `luxe.bundle_products` metafield.
- **Automatic**: If no metafield is set, it will pull products from the same collection.

### 4. Recently Viewed Products
**What it is**: Reminds customers of what they've seen.
**How to use**: Add the "Recently Viewed" section to your product pages or homepage via the Theme Editor.

## ⏱️ Urgency & Social Proof

### 5. Inventory Counter
Show real-time stock levels. You can customize the "Low Stock" threshold in the Product Page section settings.

### 6. Live Viewers & Sold Counts
Simulate or display actual social proof. These can be configured via metafields:
- `luxe.sold_count`: Number of items sold recently.
- `luxe.active_viewers`: Number of people currently viewing the product.

### 7. Flash Sale Timer
Add a countdown timer to your homepage or product pages to highlight limited-time offers. Use the **Flash Sale Timer** section.

## 🗺️ Internationalization & Localization

### 8. India-Specific Features
- **Pincode Checker**: Allows customers to check delivery availability.
- **COD Badge**: Highlights Cash on Delivery availability.
- **EMI Calculator**: Breaks down prices into manageable monthly payments.

### 9. Multi-Currency & Language
Fully compatible with Shopify Markets for a global shopping experience.
